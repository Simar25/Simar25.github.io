Patent filed

Ambient Vibe Intelligence System

An edge-AI IoT device that scores the atmosphere (“vibe”) of hospitality venues in real time.

  • Fuses lighting, occupancy, scene-aesthetic and infrastructure signals from a Raspberry Pi camera into one live score.
  • Runs YOLOv8-nano (occupancy) and MobileNetV3 (scene type) on the device itself.
  • Serves scores through a REST API to an artist–venue matching platform that connects restaurants with local musicians.
  • Working end-to-end prototype (TRL-3); patent disclosure filed through VIT Chennai.

FilingLens

An AI analyst for company annual reports: ask a question in plain English and get an answer backed by the report text and the numbers, with citations.

  • Data pipeline loads 10-K filings, XBRL financials and daily stock prices (SEC EDGAR, Yahoo Finance) into DuckDB, every figure traceable to its filing.
  • Parsed 84 reports into 1,862 sections and 21,005 passages, embedded with bge-base-en-v1.5 into ChromaDB.
  • RAG pipeline answers with numbered citations. Next: text-to-SQL, a query router, and a “Lazy Prices” study of report wording vs. stock returns.

Customer Churn Prediction

Predicts which telecom customers are about to leave, and explains why.

  • IBM Telco dataset: 7,043 customers, 26% churners. SMOTE applied to the training split only, so no data leakage.
  • Compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost on recall and ROC-AUC rather than accuracy (best AUC 0.83).
  • SHAP shows short tenure and month-to-month contracts drive churn. Deployed as an interactive Streamlit app.
