01Patent filed

Ambient Vibe Intelligence System

A small edge-AI device that tells a restaurant or venue what its atmosphere is like right now.

A Raspberry Pi camera combines lighting, how full the room is, the look of the space and infrastructure signals into a live “vibe” score. A matching platform uses that score to pair venues with local musicians. There is a working end-to-end prototype (TRL-3), and the patent disclosure was filed through VIT Chennai.

02In progress

FilingLens

An AI analyst for company annual reports. Ask a question in plain English and get an answer drawn from the report and the numbers, with citations.

The data pipeline pulls 10-K filings, XBRL financial statements and daily stock prices from SEC EDGAR and Yahoo Finance into DuckDB, and every figure can be traced back to its filing. Reports are split into sections and passages, embedded, and searched by a RAG pipeline that answers with numbered citations. Next up: text-to-SQL for the numbers, and a small replication of the “Lazy Prices” paper on whether changes in report wording predict stock returns.

Role
Solo project
Built with
Python, DuckDB, ChromaDB, bge-base-en-v1.5, Gemini, pytest

03Live demo

Customer Churn Prediction

Predicts which telecom customers are about to cancel, and explains why.

Trained on the IBM Telco dataset: 7,043 customers, 26% of whom churned. I balanced the classes with SMOTE on the training split only, so nothing leaked into the test set, and compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost on recall and ROC-AUC instead of accuracy. The best model reached a ROC-AUC of 0.83, and it runs as a Streamlit app.

Built with
pandas, scikit-learn, imbalanced-learn, XGBoost, SHAP, Streamlit

05Team project

Campus Finder

A lost-and-found system for a college campus, built for our database course.

Students report lost or found items, search every report and claim what is theirs. The interesting part is matching: a single SQL self-join scores every lost–found pair out of 100 on category, location, date and description, and surfaces the likely matches.

Built with
SQLite, SQL, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
